# 🛡️ Shield Insurance – Power BI Business Intelligence Dashboard

> **Turning Insurance Data into Actionable Business Insights**

An interactive **Power BI Business Intelligence Dashboard** developed for the **Shield Insurance** case study as part of the **Codebasics Virtual Internship**.

The project transforms insurance data into meaningful business insights across revenue, customers, sales channels, demographics, policy preferences, and regional performance.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

Shield Insurance required a centralized analytics solution to monitor business performance and understand the factors driving revenue and customer growth.

The dashboard was designed to help stakeholders:

- Monitor key business KPIs
- Track revenue and customer trends
- Analyze sales channel performance
- Understand customer demographics
- Compare regional performance
- Analyze policy preferences
- Identify business growth opportunities

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

The business needed a better way to analyze and monitor its insurance operations.

Key requirements included:

- Centralized monitoring of business performance
- Revenue and customer growth tracking
- Sales channel performance analysis
- Customer segmentation by age group and city
- Policy trend and preference analysis
- Interactive filtering across business dimensions
- Faster and more data-driven decision-making

---

## 💡 Project Objective

Develop an interactive Power BI dashboard that enables stakeholders to:

- Monitor Revenue and Customer KPIs
- Analyze revenue and customer trends over time
- Compare Offline and Online sales channels
- Understand customer demographics
- Analyze city-wise business performance
- Study policy preferences across age groups
- Analyze expected settlement values
- Explore data using interactive filters

---

# 📊 Dashboard Structure

The solution consists of four main sections.

### 🏠 Home Page

A navigation-focused landing page providing access to the major analytical sections:

- General View
- Sales Analysis
- Age Analysis

---

### 📈 General View – Executive Overview

Provides a high-level view of overall business performance.

**Key analysis:**

- Total Revenue
- Total Customers
- Daily Revenue Growth
- Daily Customer Growth
- Monthly Revenue Trend
- City-wise Revenue Performance
- Customer Distribution by Age Group
- Customer Segmentation

**Key Insight:**

The business generated **₹13.21M in revenue from 340 customers** during the analysis period.

**March 2023** recorded the highest monthly revenue, while **Delhi NCR** emerged as the highest revenue-contributing city.

The **31–45 years** age group represents the largest customer segment.

---

### 💼 Sales Analysis

Analyzes the performance of different sales channels.

**Sales Channels:**

- Offline Agent
- Offline Direct
- Online App
- Online Website

**Key analysis:**

- Customer Split %
- Revenue Split %
- Revenue by Sales Mode
- Monthly Sales Mode Trends
- Offline vs Online Performance

**Key Insight:**

**Offline Agent** is the strongest sales channel in terms of customer and revenue contribution.

Digital channels provide an opportunity to increase online customer acquisition and diversify the sales mix.

---

### 👥 Age Analysis

Provides deeper insights into customer demographics and behavior.

**Key analysis:**

- Customers by Age Group
- Customer Trends by Age Group
- Expected Settlement by Age Group
- Policy Preference by Age Group
- Age Group vs Sales Mode

**Key Insight:**

The **31–45 years** segment represents the largest customer group.

The analysis also highlights differences in policy preferences and sales channel usage across age groups, creating opportunities for more targeted marketing and product strategies.

---

# 🔍 Key Business Insights

### 📈 Business Performance

- Generated **₹13.21M** in total revenue from **340 customers**.
- The dashboard provides a centralized view of key business KPIs.

### 📊 Revenue Trend

- Revenue remained relatively stable during the analysis period.
- **March 2023** recorded the highest monthly revenue.

### 🌍 Regional Performance

- **Delhi NCR** emerged as the highest revenue-contributing city.
- Lower-performing regions present opportunities for expansion and customer acquisition.

### 👥 Customer Demographics

- The **31–45 years** age group represents the largest customer segment.
- This segment provides strong potential for targeted insurance offerings.

### 💼 Sales Performance

- **Offline Agent** contributes the largest share of customers and revenue.
- Digital sales channels present opportunities for further growth.

---

# 📈 Business Recommendations

### 1. Strengthen High-Performing Markets

Continue focusing on high-performing regions such as **Delhi NCR**, while developing strategies to improve performance in lower-contributing cities.

### 2. Expand Digital Sales

Maintain the strong performance of Offline Agents while investing in **Online App and Online Website** channels to improve digital customer acquisition.

### 3. Target the 31–45 Age Group

Develop targeted campaigns and personalized policy offerings for the **31–45 years** segment.

### 4. Enable Continuous Performance Monitoring

Use the dashboard as a centralized decision-support tool to monitor KPIs, identify emerging trends, and support faster business decisions.

# 🗂️ Data Model

The Power BI solution uses a structured data model consisting of:

### Fact Tables

- `fact_premiums`
- `fact_settlements`

### Dimension Tables

- `dim_customer`
- `dim_policies`
- `dim_date`

The model connects transactional insurance data with customer, policy, date, and settlement information to support interactive analysis and DAX calculations.
